Understanding Fake News vs Real News

Discover how misinformation spreads, how to identify deceitful patterns, and how our neural RoBERTa model uncovers truth.

Anatomy of Real News

Credible & Verified
  • Named Sources & Attribution: Cites specific researchers, institutional spokespersons, and peer-reviewed journals.
  • Objective, Non-Sensational Tone: Uses neutral phrasing without excessive capitalization, exclamation marks, or manufactured rage.
  • Verifiable Data & Corrections: Published by accountable news outlets with editorial standards and retraction transparency.

Anatomy of Fake News

Misleading & Fabricated
  • Clickbait & Panic Framing: Uses urgent triggers like 'SHOCKING!', 'THEY DONT WANT YOU TO KNOW', and all-caps shouting.
  • Unverified Miracle / Conspiracy Claims: Makes extraordinary scientific or political claims without peer review or official records.
  • Manipulative Call-to-Action: Urges readers to 'Share before it gets deleted!' to exploit emotional algorithmic engagement.

Crucial Model Limitations & Transparency Notice

This system uses a 3-signal architecture combining neural RoBERTa linguistic analysis, structured attribution checks, and real-time live web corroboration. Neural language models evaluate writing style and structure, not empirical physical truth.

Web Corroboration Scope: Web verification cross-references reputable journalistic and academic domains (Reuters, AP, BBC, NASA, Nature, CDC, etc.). Web corroboration works best for established or previously-reported claims; very recent breaking news may show lower confidence until indexed by search engines. Always cross-check critical news with primary source documents.
